**Finding 2**
- `severity`: blocker
- `위치`: [service.ts](/Users/satgym/work/harness/examples/starpin/backend/src/iss/service.ts:81)
- `근거`: `position(observerDate?)` computes geocentric RA/Dec from the ISS ECI/TEME vector and accepts no observer location. For a LEO object, “where the ISS is in the sky” is strongly observer-dependent; topocentric parallax is not a small precession-scale correction. This is unlike stars/planets where geocentric RA/Dec is acceptable for the current UI tolerance.
- `제안`: Change the route/service contract to accept observer lat/lon/alt and compute observer-topocentric apparent direction from satellite ECF minus observer ECF, then convert that look vector to RA/Dec or directly to alt/az for the existing viewport. Keep geodetic subpoint as a separate debug field.
